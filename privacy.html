Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Easy Spanish News uses Google OAuth and Google Drive.

Google OAuth

Easy Spanish News uses Google OAuth to let you authorize access to your Google Drive. Authentication is handled by Google. The application does not ask for your Google password.

Google Drive

The application uses Google Drive to store and manage files generated by Easy Spanish News. Google Drive remains subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Use of information

Access to Google Drive is used only for the application's stated purpose of handling generated files. Easy Spanish News does not use Google Drive content for advertising, sell it, or share it with third parties for unrelated purposes.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The current version is published on this page.
