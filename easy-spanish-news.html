Application overview

Easy Spanish News

Easy Spanish News creates simple Spanish news content and videos for people learning Spanish.

What the application does

The application helps create Spanish news material in an accessible format for language learners. It also uses Google Drive to store and manage the files it generates.

Google Drive access

Easy Spanish News uses Google OAuth so you can authorize the application to work with Google Drive. Access is used for the application's file-storage and file-management purpose.
